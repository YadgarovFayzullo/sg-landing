For universities · 2 months

“Startup foundation” offline intensive

Builds venture thinking in students: spotting a problem, solving it with a startup approach and presenting an idea convincingly.

2 monthsduration
2×22 days a week, 2 hours a day
8week program
20–30selected students

Selection

Online test: 20 questions, 15–20 minutes, maximum 100 points. Top scorers are admitted, the rest are placed on a waitlist.

What they learn

  • Defining the problem
  • Business model
  • MVP
  • Go to market
  • Finance and legal basics
  • Team and leadership
  • Pitching

8-week program

  1. 1Entrepreneurial mindset, problem and idea validation
  2. 2Business model, value proposition, competition
  3. 3Building and testing the MVP
  4. 4GTM and first customers
  5. 5Unit economics and fundraising
  6. 6Legal basics, team, operations
  7. 7Pitch deck and storytelling
  8. 8Final preparation and Demo Day

Student gets

  • A startup idea based on a real problem
  • MVP concept and pitch deck
  • Defense at Demo Day and certificate

Included in the program

  • Trainer/mentor and individual feedback
  • Templates: MVP, pitch deck, business model
  • Option to continue SG residency
