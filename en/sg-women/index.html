About SG Women

SG Women is an initiative by Startup Garage to inspire, educate and support women founders at every stage of the startup journey.

  • 01

    A community where you feel free

    Nobody here laughs at your experience or your questions. Every member is valued, heard and supported.

  • 02

    From idea to practice

    Not just inspiration — real action. You get hands-on help building a startup from scratch and putting it into practice.

  • 03

    Local roots, global opportunity

    We want the voice of Uzbek women to be heard — not only here, but all over the world.

  • 04

    You are not alone

    Here are women who understand your path and share your dreams. We learn together and move forward together.

A startup space for women

Have a new idea? Not sure how to start? We support exactly such women — we are by your side as you turn your idea into a strong business.

Our masterclasses are not just theory — women who found their own path share their experience.

What we offer

An ideal environment for sharing experience, learning and building strong partnerships. From the first steps to financial support — we are with you at every stage.

  • Mentoring programs

    A chance to work with industry experts. Personal guidance on startups, business or technology.

  • MVP and financial support

    Strategic help in turning an idea into a working product. Preparation for raising investment.

  • Networking events

    Meet people with goals, find partners and open the door to new opportunities.

  • Workshops and masterclasses

    New skills through hands-on practice. Work with experts and gain real experience.
