What is Grand Demo Day?

Grand Demo Day is a major event where promising startups present their projects to investors, business representatives and a wide audience. It is the final stage of an eight-week startup selection.

During Grand Demo Day, selected startup founders showcase their products, business models, results achieved and future development plans.

Grand Demo Day is not just a presentation. It is a place for startups to build new partnerships, attract investor attention, create strategic connections and turn their ideas into real business opportunities.

Grand Demo Day is where ideas become opportunities, and opportunities become real projects.

What awaits you at the event:

  • Presentations by promising startups
  • Meetings with venture funds and investors
  • Q&A and professional discussions
  • Networking and new partnership opportunities
  • Meeting representatives of the startup ecosystem
