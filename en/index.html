    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions.

    What is Startup Garage?

    Startup Garage is an intensive, project-based program that equips startups with the tools, knowledge and support they need to accelerate growth. Inspired by Stanford University's well-known Startup Garage program.

    Who can apply to Startup Garage?

    Anyone with entrepreneurial drive and an innovative business idea. We accept entrepreneurs from all industries and from across Central Asia.

    How long is the program?

    The Residency program is designed to turn your startup into a sustainable business over 18 months. The bootcamp covers every stage of the startup journey — from ideation to going to market.

    What topics does the program cover?

    Startup fundamentals, product development, product launch, finance and fundraising, marketing and sales, operations and management.

    Is there a fee to join?

    Yes, there is a fee to join the program. However, to support promising entrepreneurs we offer various funding opportunities and scholarships.

    What does the selection process look like?

    The initial application is reviewed, then shortlisted candidates are interviewed. Your business idea, entrepreneurial potential and readiness for the program are assessed.

    Can we apply as a team?

    Yes, you can apply individually or as a team. If you apply as a team, include information about all members in the application.

    What happens after the program ends?

    Participants get the chance to present their business to potential investors. Graduates receive ongoing support and resources.
